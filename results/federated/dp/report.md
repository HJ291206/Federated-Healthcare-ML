# Differential-privacy experiment

> Clients are simulated partitions of a single public survey dataset (CDC BRFSS 2015). They are not hospitals or real institutions, and federated learning alone does not guarantee privacy: model updates can still leak information about training data.

Generated by `python -m ml.federated.dp_experiment --config configs/federated_dp.yaml` on 2026-10-15 02:44:19 +0530. All numbers are read from the CSV files in this folder.

## What is and is not claimed

- **Non-private rows** are ordinary FedAvg/FedProx. Raw rows stay on the client (data locality), but the released model updates carry **no formal privacy guarantee**.
- **Private rows** use example-level DP-SGD (Opacus 1.6.0): Poisson sampling, per-example L2 clipping to C = 1.0, Gaussian noise with σ·C standard deviation, RDP accounting. The **privacy unit is one training example of one client**. ε is reported per client at δ = 1e-05 and summarized by the maximum over clients.
- This is **not client-level DP**: it does not hide whether a whole client participated.
- Hyperparameters were not tuned under DP, and the noise RNG is not cryptographically secure (`secure_mode=False`).
